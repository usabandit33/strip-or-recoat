About this site

Strip Or Recoat is a free checklist for the Phoenix metro. It helps a facility manager ask whether a commercial floor quote is a scrub-and-recoat or a full strip-and-wax.

Phone numbers are copied from each company’s own public website. The list is not a ranking, referral, or endorsement. Shops are not charged a listing fee on the emails this factory sends in touches 1–4.

You never pay this site to read the checklist or to call a published number.
