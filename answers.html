Common questions

Is a scrub and recoat the same as a strip and wax?

No. A scrub and recoat cleans the surface and adds finish. A full strip removes the old finish before new coats go down. Ask which one is on the quote.

Why do two Phoenix floor quotes look so different?

One quote may be burnish or recoat. Another may be a full strip, more coats, furniture moving, or after-hours work. Ask for that scope in writing. This site does not set prices.

Does a yellow floor always need a full strip?

Not always. Ask whether the color is embedded soil that a scrub can lift, or finish that has to come off. Get the reason in writing.

Should I ask about coat count?

Yes. Ask how many coats are in the price and whether a burnish is included or extra.
