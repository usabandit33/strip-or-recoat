Before you book a Phoenix floor crew, ask if the quote is a recoat or a full strip.

Two shops can both say “wax the floor.” One means a scrub and a new coat on top. The other means stripping the old finish off VCT, then building coats back. Those are different jobs, different downtime, and different invoices.

  1. Name the floor: VCT, sheet vinyl, LVT, or sealed concrete.
  2. Ask if the quote is burnish only, scrub-and-recoat, or a full strip.
  3. Ask coat count, furniture move, and whether the crew works after hours.
  4. Call a number published on the shop’s own site and get that scope in writing.
